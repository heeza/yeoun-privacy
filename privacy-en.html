Yeoun Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026 (draft written 2026-11-11)

Operator: Yeoun developer (hyuns) · Contact: cosmos6464@gmail.com

Yeoun ("the app") is a personal journaling app that brings your past thoughts back to you. This policy states exactly what the app keeps, where, and when anything leaves your phone.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or login. The operator runs no server and never receives or stores what you write.
  • Your notes are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Your writing leaves the phone in one case only: when you connect Gemini yourself and tap the Gemini question button.

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of the above. You can delete individual notes in the app, and you can delete the Deep AI file in Settings.

3. When data leaves your device

3-1. Gemini questions (optional, off by default)

  • This works only after you connect your own Google AI Studio API key under "Connect Gemini" in Settings.
  • Each time you tap the Gemini button, the first 500 characters of the text you were writing, plus an instruction asking it to create a question, are sent over HTTPS to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com).
  • This transfer takes place under your own API relationship with Google; the operator does not receive the content. Google handles it under the Gemini API Terms and the Google Privacy Policy (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms, https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google's servers may be located outside Korea (for example, in the United States). Items transferred: the first 500 characters of your text. Time of transfer: when you tap the button. Recipient: Google LLC. How to refuse: do not connect Gemini, or disconnect it in Settings (the key is deleted). Nothing is then sent, and all other features remain available.
  • To verify the key, a short test sentence is sent once when you connect.
  • While the Gemini connect dialog is open and you return from AI Studio, the app checks the clipboard for text shaped like a key (AIza…) and fills it in. It does not read the clipboard in any other case.

3-2. Downloading AI model files (optional)

  • The small AI (Qwen3-0.6B) and the deep AI (Gemma 4 E2B) are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) only after you agree and tap Download. Only the file request is sent; your notes are never sent. As with ordinary internet requests, connection data such as your IP address may be logged by Hugging Face.
  • Downloaded models run only on this phone. Text used to generate questions does not leave the phone.

3-3. Gemini Nano (on-device AI)

  • On supported phones, questions are generated on the device through Google's ML Kit GenAI (Gemini Nano, the system's AICore). Your writing does not leave the device.
  • The ML Kit SDK may send Google diagnostic data such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, error codes, and the configured language, for product improvement and error diagnosis (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). Your notes are not included.

3-4. Voice input

  • The Voice tab opens the speech-recognition app installed on your phone (for example, Google). The app receives only the recognized text and never receives or stores a recording. Speech processing follows that speech-recognition app's privacy policy.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos, account information, or payment information. We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Retention and deletion

Notes stay only on this phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. The operator holds no personal information, so there is no separate destruction procedure. Inquiry emails are deleted within one year after we reply.

7. Provision to third parties and outsourcing

The operator does not provide personal information to third parties or outsource its processing. The transfers in section 3 are features you chose yourself.

8. Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children (in particular, children under 14). The Gemini connection follows Google AI Studio's terms of use (18+).

9. Your rights

You can view, edit, and delete your notes directly in the app. The operator does not have your notes, so there is no information to request from the operator. For questions, please contact us at the address below.

10. Security measures

Notes and settings are kept in Android app-private storage so other apps cannot read them. External transfers use HTTPS only. The Gemini key is sent in a request header, not in the request URL.
