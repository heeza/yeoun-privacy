Yeoun Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026

Operator: Yeoun developer (hyuns) · Contact: cosmos6464@gmail.com

Yeoun ("the app") is a personal journaling app that brings your past thoughts back to you. This policy states exactly what the app keeps, where, and when anything leaves your phone.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or login. The operator runs no server and never receives or stores what you write.
  • Your notes are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Nothing sends the text of your notes off the phone automatically. It leaves only when you choose: (1) when you connect Gemini and tap the Gemini question button (3-1), or (2) when you save or share an export, encrypted backup, or the This year of Yeoun PDF yourself (3-5).
  • Voice input, Google Play billing, on-device AI (ML Kit), and model downloads can send information other than your note text off the device. All of these are listed in section 3.

3. When data leaves your device

3-1. Gemini questions (optional, off by default)

  • This works only after you connect your own Google AI Studio API key under "Connect Gemini" in Settings.
  • Each time you tap the Gemini button, the first 500 characters of the text you were writing, plus an instruction asking it to create a question, are sent over HTTPS to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com).
  • This transfer takes place under your own API relationship with Google; the operator does not receive the content. Google handles it under the Gemini API Terms and the Google Privacy Policy (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms, https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • With a free-tier key, Google may use the text you send to improve its products, and people may review it. Do not send sensitive text with the Gemini button. Paid-tier keys are handled under the Gemini API Terms.
  • Google's servers may be located outside Korea (for example, in the United States). Items transferred: the first 500 characters of your text. Time of transfer: when you tap the button. Recipient: Google LLC. How to refuse: do not connect Gemini, or disconnect it in Settings (the key is deleted). Nothing is then sent, and all other features remain available.
  • To verify the key, a short test sentence is sent once when you connect.
  • While the Gemini connect dialog is open and you return from AI Studio, the app checks the clipboard for text shaped like a key (AIza…) and fills it in. It does not read the clipboard in any other case.

3-2. Downloading AI model files (optional)

  • The small AI (Qwen3-0.6B) and the deep AI (Gemma 4 E2B) are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) only after you agree and tap Download. Only the file request is sent; your notes are never sent. As with ordinary internet requests, connection data such as your IP address may be logged by Hugging Face.
  • Downloaded models run only on this phone, and the text used to create questions does not leave the phone.

3-3. Gemini Nano (on-device AI)

  • On supported phones, questions are created on the device with Google's ML Kit GenAI (Gemini Nano, through the system's AICore). Your writing does not leave the device.
  • The ML Kit SDK may send diagnostic information to Google, such as device and app information, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, error codes, event types, and the device language, to improve the feature and diagnose errors (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). It does not include the content of your notes.

3-4. Voice input

  • The Voice tab opens the speech recognition app installed on your phone (for example, Google). That app processes your voice, and depending on its settings it may recognize speech on the device or on its company's servers. Yeoun receives only the recognized text and never receives or stores a recording. Voice processing follows that app's privacy policy.

3-5. Export, encrypted backup, and This year of Yeoun PDF (only when you do it)

  • Export notes (JSON) and Export a readable copy (Markdown) save a file to a location you choose. These files are not encrypted, so keep them carefully.
  • Yeoun+ encrypted backup locks your notes with a passphrase you set (Argon2id, AES-256-GCM) and saves them to a folder you choose. If you forget the passphrase, the operator cannot open the backup either.
  • The This year of Yeoun PDF is made on the device, and you choose where to share it.
  • If the folder you chose syncs with a service such as Google Drive, or you share a file with another app, that file is moved and kept under that service's or app's privacy policy. The operator never receives these files.

3-6. Yeoun+ payment (if purchased)

  • Yeoun+ payments are processed by Google Play. Google Play receives your payment details, such as your card number; the operator and the app never receive them. Google Play handles them under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • To confirm and restore purchases, the app talks to Google Play through the Google Play Billing Library and keeps the purchase token only on this phone. In the Google Play Console, the operator can see transaction records such as the order number, country, and amount (other details follow Google Play policy). They are used only for refunds and tax.

3-7. Reporting an AI question (optional)

  • If you tap Report under a question written by AI, your mail app opens with a draft containing only that question and which AI wrote it. Your writing is not included. It reaches the operator only if you tap Send, and the email is deleted within one year after we reply.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not collect your advertising ID, location, contacts, photos, or account information. The operator never receives payment details such as card numbers. The app uses no advertising, analytics, or crash reporting SDKs.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your notes stay only on this phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • How to delete: delete individual notes in the app. Disconnecting Gemini in Settings deletes the key. The Deep AI file can be deleted in Settings. The small AI file has no separate delete button in the app yet; it is removed when you uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything in section 2.
  • Files you exported or backed up, and PDFs you shared, stay where you put them; delete them there.
  • Information already delivered to Google (Gemini API, ML Kit, Google Play) or to a speech recognition app cannot be deleted by the operator. Please follow each company's process, for example your Google Account data controls (https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).
  • The only personal information the operator keeps is support email, deleted within one year after we reply. Transaction records stay with Google Play for the period set by Google Play and applicable law.

7. Sharing and processors

The operator does not provide personal information to third parties or outsource its processing. The transfers in section 3 are features you choose, or take place between you and the company concerned (such as Google).

8. Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children (especially those under 14). Connecting Gemini is subject to the Google AI Studio terms (18 and over).

9. Your rights

You can view, edit, delete, and export your notes directly in the app. The operator does not hold your notes, so there is nothing to request from the operator. For questions, contact us below.

10. Security

Notes and settings are kept in Android's app-private storage, where other apps cannot read them. External transfers use HTTPS only. The Gemini key is sent in a header, not in the request URL. Encrypted backups are locked with a passphrase stretched by Argon2id and AES-256-GCM, and the passphrase for automatic backup is locked with the Android Keystore.
